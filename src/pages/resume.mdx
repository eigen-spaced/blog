This systematic approach eliminated compliance gaps while maintaining the confidentiality and accuracy standards required for official student records.

### Process Digitization Initiative

Manual paperwork processes were creating inefficiencies and potential compliance issues across multiple departments. I led digitization efforts that streamlined data collection workflows, reducing overall paperwork by 16% while strengthening compliance controls. This transition improved both operational efficiency and data integrity by eliminating manual transcription errors.

### IT Systems Advocacy & Implementation

The organization lacked systematic tracking for technical issues, creating accountability gaps and inefficient resolution processes. I identified this operational risk and advocated for implementing a ticketing system, contributing to its successful adoption. This change improved issue tracking, established clear accountability measures, and created audit trails for technical support activities.

### Excel Automation for Asset Tracking

The institution was managing multiple asset tracking spreadsheets (inventory, building access cards, and other resources) that were prone to inconsistencies and missing data due to manual double-entry. I redesigned these systems using formulas and linked tables to automate data updates across multiple tracking categories. When items were checked out or returned, one entry would automatically update master inventories, eliminating the need for separate manual updates and significantly reducing tracking errors across all asset types.

### Attendance System Digital Transformation

Trainers were submitting paper attendance sheets that front desk staff had to manually enter into the system, creating bottlenecks and errors. I pushed through a digital transition that had been talked about but never implemented, creating training materials and providing hands-on support during rollout. This eliminated the manual data entry burden by 30% and improved attendance accuracy since trainers became directly responsible for their own data.

### IT Support & Student Communications Management
The college's support desk was handling ad-hoc technology issues and student inquiries without standardized processes, leading to inconsistent response times and communication gaps. I established systematic troubleshooting workflows for classroom technology, printers, and device connectivity while developing templated communication protocols for student follow-ups and course support. This dual approach reduced repeat tickets by creating clearer initial guidance and improved student satisfaction through more professional, consistent messaging across all touchpoints.

## AI-Powered Pairwise Ranking App
- Most ranking systems break down when comparing fundamentally different categories—how do you fairly rank Batman against a Toyota Corolla? I built an application that uses ELO rating algorithms to handle these "apples to oranges" comparisons, revealing unexpected patterns in human preference hierarchies.
- The system generates a dynamic network graph showing relationship clusters between unlikely items (Nicolas Cage cardboard cutouts vs. wet socks vs. the Ghostbusters film), creating a visual map of how people actually make irrational comparison decisions. This exploration into comparative psychology demonstrates how statistical models can extract meaningful insights from seemingly nonsensical data.

>Technical highlights: ELO rating implementation, dynamic graph visualization, statistical analysis of irrational decision patterns.
