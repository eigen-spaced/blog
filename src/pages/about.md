I’m a developer who refuses to stay in a single lane. Equally at home debugging code, dissecting culture, or debating the finer points of compliance frameworks. My journey now weaves through Governance, Risk & Compliance, and Information Security, where technology meets trust and resilience.

Outside of work, I move between high-intensity sports, reading whatever shows up on my radar, meditative yoga flows, and long dives into spirituality and the shifting tides of modern culture. Whether it’s code, policy, or philosophy, I’m drawn to systems—how they work, how they fail, and how to make them better.

This blog is where I explore all of that: tech and security, sports and movement, culture and consciousness. If it challenges the mind or body, it probably shows up here.
